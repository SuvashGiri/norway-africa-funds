# Hard Currency, Local Finance

How Norway's development finance reaches African businesses, and who carries the currency risk.

**Live report:** https://suvashgiri.github.io/norway-africa-funds/


## What it covers
- Where a dollar loan would have broken: a map of how far each currency fell against the USD at its worst since November 2021, against Norfund's commitments
- Norfund's public portfolio by country and contract currency (Africa outside South Africa)
- Two structures where financing reaches local businesses in local currency: Ghana and Sun King (Kenya)
- An interactive DSCR calculator for a kwacha-earning SME in Zambia: how far the currency can fall before debt service breaks
- Context from NBIM's public holdings

## Method and limits
Built from public sources only: Norfund's investment overview workbook, Norfund and company announcements, daily exchange rates from ValutaFX and Pound Sterling Live, and NBIM's holdings data. Country shares are reconstructed at instrument level. The DSCR calculator uses illustrative assumptions, not market quotes. See the report for full method, sources and limits.

Independent project by Suvash Giri, built with an AI assistant. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Norfund or NBIM.
